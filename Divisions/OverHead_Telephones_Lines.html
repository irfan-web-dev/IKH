Our Line Services

Complete Overhead Solutions

IKH Groups specializes in the design, installation, and maintenance of overhead power lines and telephone networks throughout Saudi Arabia. Our services cover everything from high-voltage transmission lines to local distribution networks and telecommunication cabling. We combine technical expertise with strict safety protocols to deliver reliable infrastructure solutions for both urban and rural applications.

Our overhead line services include complete turnkey installations of new power distribution networks, upgrades of existing systems, and routine maintenance programs. We work with all types of conductors and poles including wooden, concrete, and steel structures. For telephone networks, we provide aerial fiber optic and copper cable installations, splicing services, and network testing. All our work complies with Saudi Electricity Company (SEC) specifications and international safety standards.

We employ specialized teams trained in live-line working techniques where required, minimizing service disruptions during maintenance and repair operations. Our services extend to overhead line surveying, pole testing and replacement, insulator cleaning, and lightning protection system installation. For telecommunication providers, we offer complete aerial network solutions including drop wire installations and last-mile connectivity.

With extensive experience in challenging environments, we've successfully completed overhead line projects in desert conditions, mountainous terrain, and urban centers. Our project management approach ensures minimal environmental impact while delivering durable infrastructure. We maintain a fleet of specialized equipment including bucket trucks, line tensioners, and testing devices to efficiently handle projects of any scale.
